Clinical training teaches you to treat the patient in front of you. It takes
longer to see the system behind them — the reasons they arrived late, the record
that did not follow them, the resources that were never there.

That system-level view is what pulled me toward the **Sustainable Development
Goals** and **United Nations Academic Impact (UNAI)**. Health outcomes are
rarely only clinical. They sit downstream of education, equity, infrastructure
and information.

## Millennium Fellowship

I served as a **Millennium Fellow** at Ebonyi State University, a leadership
programme convened by the Millennium Campus Network in partnership with UNAI,
which supports student-led projects advancing the SDGs on their own campuses.

## Why this sits alongside the data work

The instinct is the same in both halves of what I do: look for where a system
loses people, and measure it honestly. In global health that means asking who is
missed by a programme. In on-chain analysis it means asking who stopped showing
up after the first week. The subject changes; the question does not.
