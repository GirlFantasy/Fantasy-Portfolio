A comparative Base vs Mantle growth study across **10M+ transactions**, analysing
activation patterns, periodic retention, economic activity and contract
interaction distribution. It showed how each L2's marketing strategy drove
significant variation in adoption and engagement, despite the two chains sharing
qualities within the same ecosystem.
