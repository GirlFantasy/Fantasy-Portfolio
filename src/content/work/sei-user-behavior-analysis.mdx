## Context

Most published chain analysis is a snapshot: a question asked once, answered
once, and left to age. Behaviour does not hold still, and a dashboard that stops
updating stops being evidence.

This project takes the opposite approach. It is a **live** analysis of user
behaviour on SEI, running since September 2025 and updated as the chain
develops.

## The question

**How do users actually behave on SEI — and how does that behaviour change over
time?**

The same line of enquiry that drives my Solana work: look past acquisition
totals to what users do after they arrive, and whether that pattern holds.

## The dashboard

The analysis is published openly on Dune, so the queries are inspectable and the
figures reproducible rather than asserted.
